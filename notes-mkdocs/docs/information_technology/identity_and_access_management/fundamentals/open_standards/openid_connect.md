This standard is built on top of OAuth 2.0. OAuth deals with permissions (what the app can view or do), while OpenID Connect deals with authentication (who you are). It adds the ID token, which is a JWT with claims about you.

This is a more modern approach and is often seen on websites and browsers (Microsoft Edge profiles).
