## Authentication

This is how you confirm you are who you say you are. You give a username, and then a password to really say "I'm not an imposter." You can also use MFA, which is another step to prove your identity.

## Authorization

When you have been authenticated by the local guard, said guard checks what you have access to. Said guard will walk you through the premises and guide you where you need to be.
