*What can I see?* Depending on the operating system (OS) I can see who logs in to the computer, who started a process, who ran a command, what command was run, etc. For example, in Windows OS you can view logs in the '*Event Viewer*'

[Windows Security Log Events](https://www.ultimatewindowssecurity.com/securitylog/encyclopedia/default.aspx)

*Why is that important?* So you can catch malicious behavior on that machine. Attackers have a lot of different ways to do recon, exploit, and pull data out, and the logs are where those actions leave traces.
