## What is ANI?

Everything we actually have today, Claude, GPT, Copilot, every commercial AI tool, is Artificial Narrow Intelligence (ANI). These models are really good at the kinds of tasks they were trained for, but they don't learn on the job. Once training is done the weights are frozen, and anything "new" they know in a conversation is just whatever is sitting in the context window.

## What is AGI?

Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) would be human-level across the board: common sense, learning new things from a few examples, and handling any domain without being retrained for it. Nobody has built it, and there isn't even one agreed-on definition. Experts argue about whether it's years away, decades away, or the wrong goal entirely.

## Why does it matter?

For my day to day it mostly doesn't. Don't let the AGI debate distract from what these tools can already do. There's a huge gap between "can do everything a human can" and "can draft my weekly Defender for Cloud summary so I just review it," and the second one is doable right now.
